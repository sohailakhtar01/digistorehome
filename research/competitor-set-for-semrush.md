# Competitor set for Semrush Keyword Gap

Derived from `raw/serp-google-2026-08-26.md`. Google SERPs, US desktop, captured
2026-08-26. Semrush's index is Google-based, so Google SERPs are the correct input
here — the Bing SERPs used for product selection are not interchangeable.

Semrush Keyword Gap accepts **you + 4 competitors**. Run it more than once with
different sets; one run cannot answer both questions below.

## Do NOT use these as Semrush competitors

Not because they are unimportant, but because their keyword profiles will produce
garbage in a gap analysis:

- `amazon.com`, `store.motherearthnews.com`, `anniesheirloomseeds.com` — marketplace
  and retail. Millions of unrelated keywords.
- `reddit.com`, `quora.com`, `medium.com`, `vocal.media`, `youtube.com`, `facebook.com` — UGC.
  They rank on domain strength, not on anything reproducible.
- `sites.google.com`, `blogmuahang.mystrikingly.com`, `dyspraxiausa.org` — free-host and
  hijacked-upload spam. Nothing to learn.
- `globenewswire.com`, `newswire.com` — paid press releases.
- `mcgill.ca`, `extension.umd.edu`, `ncsu.edu`, `ufl.edu`, `botanicgardens.org` — `.edu`
  and institutional. Cannot be competed with on authority and should not be tried.
- `nicoleapelian.com` — the vendor. We are their affiliate, not their competitor.

## What the SERP composition actually says

**The money term is weak, and weak in an unusual way.** Of eighteen results for
"medicinal garden kit review", exactly one — `naturalherbgarden.com` at #15 — is a
purpose-built affiliate review. The rest is UGC, paid PR, free-host spam, a PDF sitting
in a WordPress uploads folder, and the vendor's own blog. There is no incumbent to
displace, which is the whole reason this product was chosen.

**The second result is a debunk.** McGill's Office for Science and Society ranks #2
with "The Medicinal Garden Kit Will Probably Not Save You". A meaningful share of
people searching this term are looking for a reason *not* to buy. A review that
pretends the criticism does not exist loses those readers to McGill and earns nothing.
Our review already documents the PhD-not-MD distinction and the three difficult
species; that is the right instinct and it should stay.

**The non-brand cluster is the harder but larger prize.** "how to grow lavender from
seed" is contested by seed retailers with real link profiles, and the first organic
result sits at absolute position 4 — three non-organic blocks sit above it. But
`nextdoorhomestead.com` holds #2 there with one blog post, which sets the bar at
"one genuinely good article", not "a retailer's domain authority".

**Where the gap analysis should point.** Neither our review nor the retailers'
guides cover failure states: seeds that did not germinate, mould during cold
stratification, year-one versus year-two flowering. Reddit and Facebook rank for
exactly those questions, which means the demand is real and no indexed article is
answering it well. That is the most likely thing Semrush will surface, and the
cheapest content to write honestly.
