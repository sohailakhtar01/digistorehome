# Where the site actually stands — 27 August 2026

Two days after launch. Everything below is measured, not estimated. Sources are
named so each line can be re-run and checked rather than believed.

## Index status

| Engine | State | How it was checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google | **13 of 23 pages indexed** | `site:` query, DataForSEO SERP API |
| Bing | **never crawled** | `GetUrlInfo`: `HttpStatus 0`, null `LastCrawledDate` |

Google finding 13 pages in two days on a domain with no history is a good
result, not a bad one. Bing not crawling at all is also not a fault: robots.txt
allows bingbot, the site answers in 0.3s, and the IndexNow key file resolves
200. Bingbot is simply conservative with a new domain nothing links to.

Not yet indexed by Google, including the page that earns:

```
/reviews/medicinal-garden-kit   <- the money page
/reviews  /guides  /guides/echinacea  /guides/calendula
/guides/chamomile  /guides/when-to-plant
/contact  /disclosure  /terms
```

The review page is technically clean — 200, `index, follow`, correct canonical,
affiliate links `sponsored nofollow`. An internal-link audit found it reachable
from 11 of the 13 indexed pages. Its absence is a trust-and-time problem typical
of an affiliate review on a two-day-old domain, **not** something a further
technical fix will move.

## The actual bottleneck

```
GetLinkCounts -> 0 links, from 0 pages
```

Zero referring domains. To a crawler this site is an island. That single number
explains both the Bing situation and the review page's absence from Google.

**More content will not fix this.** 23 pages, 13 indexed, zero links: page 24
does not change the arithmetic. This is the trap to avoid, because writing is
the easy part and the one that feels like progress.

## The ceiling on this product

Full branded cluster, DataForSEO `keyword_suggestions`, US, 27 Aug 2026 —
18 variants, **320 searches/month in total**:

```
medicinal garden kit           110   HIGH   RD 4
medicinal garden kit reviews    40   LOW    RD 0
medicinal garden kit review     40   LOW    RD 0
medicinal herb garden kit       30   HIGH   RD 0
... 14 more at 10 or below
```

There is no `scam`, `legit`, `complaints` or `worth it` variant at any volume.
Bottom-funnel branded content is therefore a dead end here, and ranking first
for everything branded would still be a small number of sales.

**The traffic has to come from the growing guides**, where the volume actually
is: `lavender seeds when to plant` 4,400/mo, `when to plant echinacea seeds`
1,300/mo, `coneflower seeds` peaking at 49,500 in April.

## What was done about it

1. **Bing Content Submission API** — `scripts/bing-submit-content.mjs` pushes
   live HTML straight into Bing's index, skipping the crawl. All 23 URLs
   submitted, 0 failed. Quota is 100/day, 500/month and was sitting unused.
   Content is fetched live immediately before sending; submitting anything
   other than what is served would be cloaking.
2. **Internal links** — `/guides` and `/about` now reach the review.
3. **Mailing list** — built and deployed, hidden until a provider key exists.

## The seasonal clock

This is the frame everything else hangs off.

```
Aug 2026   now — guides indexing, no links
Oct-Dec    stratification season; readers act months before they buy
Feb-Apr    the seed-buying wave. coneflower 9,900 (Dec) -> 49,500 (Apr)
```

Roughly five months of runway. Enough to build authority if link acquisition
starts now; wasted if it is spent writing more guides nobody can find.

## Next, in order of leverage

1. **Inbound links.** Requires a human and cannot be automated without becoming
   the spam we are avoiding. The most linkable asset is the frost-date
   calculator — people link to free tools, not to affiliate reviews.
2. **Switch the mailing list on.** One env var; see below.
3. **Re-check Bing in 5-7 days** — `GetUrlInfo` on the homepage. If
   `LastCrawledDate` is still null, content submission did not take and the
   answer is links, not another submission.
4. **Product #2.** The 320/mo ceiling is the reason this matters. Must clear
   the retail filter: not sold on Amazon/Walmart/Etsy/eBay, brand does not own
   the top four results.

## Switching the mailing list on

Create a free MailerLite or Kit account, then in Vercel:

```
MAILERLITE_API_KEY=...        (optionally MAILERLITE_GROUP_ID)
  or
KIT_API_KEY=...               (optionally KIT_FORM_ID)
```

Then **redeploy** — `EmailCaptureSlot` is a server component, so the check
happens at build time and setting the variable alone will not reveal the form.

## Measurement blind spot

robots.txt blocks `AhrefsBot`, `SemrushBot` and `MJ12bot`. This does not affect
rankings, but links earned will not appear in Semrush or Ahrefs, so link-building
progress cannot be tracked there. Worth unblocking if that tracking is wanted.

## The $10,000 question, answered with arithmetic

Asked what it takes to reach $10,000. The honest answer changes the strategy,
so it is recorded here rather than left as encouragement.

**Ranking first for every branded term this product has:**

```
branded searches/month           320   (all 18 variants, measured)
visits at 30% CTR                 96
sales at 5% (high buying intent)  4.8/month

  at $20/sale   $96/month    105 months to $10k
  at $30/sale  $144/month     70 months to $10k
  at $40/sale  $192/month     53 months to $10k
```

Four to nine years. Adding informational guide traffic roughly halves it and
still leaves years. This is not a conversion problem or a link problem — the
funnel work done on 27 August makes the ceiling arrive sooner, it does not
raise it. **A single $59 product with a 320/month branded market cannot reach
$10,000 in a sensible timeframe.**

### The three levers

```
334 sales x $30    the current path
 67 sales x $150   higher-ticket product
 34 sales x $300   higher-ticket, or recurring
```

Commission per sale is the strongest lever, then number of products. Traffic
is necessary for all of them and is gated on links either way.

### Why keyword tools cannot find product two

A keyword_ideas sweep across eight homesteading seeds (aquaponics, chicken coop
plans, off-grid solar, root cellar, food dehydrating, beekeeping, raised beds,
rainwater harvesting) returned nothing usable: `chicken coop` at 246,000/mo
with RD 0 is Tractor Supply and Amazon; `grow a garden pets` at 9,900 is a
Roblox game; several results were automotive. The RD mirage again — retail
category pages hold few page-level links, so the difficulty score flatters a
SERP no affiliate can enter.

The binding constraint is **distribution, not demand**. This product is
winnable because it has no Amazon listing. That is a property of the product,
discoverable in an affiliate marketplace, not in a keyword tool. Search the
marketplace first, then test demand — not the other way round.

### The filter for product two

1. Commission **$50+ per sale**, ideally $100+ (the strongest lever)
2. **No Amazon, Walmart, Etsy or eBay listing** — check by searching the name
3. Brand does **not** own the top four organic results
4. No shopping carousel on the head term
5. Search demand above ~500/month for the review cluster

Gates 2-4 are the three-gate test that has now rejected six of six candidates.
Gate 1 is new, and it is the one that decides whether $10k takes one year or
seven.

### Realistic path

Five products at $200/month each is $1,000/month — $10k inside a year once
authority exists. One product at $150/month is not a business. The site was
built as `/reviews/<product>` rather than a single-product page, so the
architecture already supports this; what is missing is the products.
