# The next plan — 4 September 2026

Day 10. Everything below is measured. Where a number is an estimate it says so.

## The finding that decides the plan

Two live SERP checks, DataForSEO, US, depth 100:

```
growing chicory                          position 85   (our best query, 9 impressions)
does feverfew need cold stratification    position 52   (our best long-tail)
```

That is the entire explanation for 366 impressions and zero clicks. Nobody
scrolls to page 6, let alone page 9.

It is worth being precise about what this is *not*. It is not thin content:
the lavender guide is 2,083 words across twelve H2s with FAQ schema. It is
not wrong keywords: Google is showing us for `growing lavender from seed`
and `growing chicory`, which is exactly what those pages are about. It is not
an indexing fault: 275 distinct queries have impressions.

Who ranks above us on `growing chicory`:

```
 2  ufseeds.com          5  sowrightseeds.com    9  gardeningknowhow.com
 4  reddit.com           6  growveg.com         10  extension.usu.edu
                         8  rhs.org.uk          20  gardenersworld.com
```

Seed retailers, the Royal Horticultural Society, a university extension
service. Domains with 15-25 years of history and thousands of referring
domains. We have **zero referring domains** — confirmed again today via Bing
`GetLinkCounts`: 0 links from 0 pages.

**No on-page work closes a 75-position gap.** Only time and links do. Any plan
that consists of writing more guides is a plan to stay on page 9.

## What follows

Three channels, ranked by when they can produce anything.

### P0 — Pinterest. Built today. Needs the account.

Pinterest ranks pins on engagement, not on domain age. It is the one place a
ten-day-old site competes with the RHS on equal terms, and gardening is one of
its largest categories. This is not a workaround for weak SEO; for this niche
it is a primary channel that happens to also be available immediately.

`npm run pins` renders **22 pins** (11 guides x 2 templates) at 1000x1500 into
`public/img/pins/`. Every fact on them is read from `herbs.js`, the same source
the guides render from, so a pin cannot claim something its page does not say.
Each guide page now carries a "Save to Pinterest" card using Pinterest's
documented save endpoint.

Seasonally this lands right. Stratification searches run September to January,
and fall sowing is now. The stratification pin is in season this week.

### P1 — Links. The only thing that unlocks Google.

Nothing else moves position 85. The most linkable asset is the frost-date
calculator, because people link to free tools and not to affiliate reviews.
This needs a human and cannot be automated without becoming spam.

### P2 — Amazon Associates. Monetises the traffic we actually get.

A reader searching `growing lavender from seed` already owns lavender seeds.
They will not buy a ten-species kit. They might buy a heat mat, a humidity
dome or a seed tray. 3-4% of a $30 order is small, but it is a real match to
real intent, where the kit is not.

**One caveat before signing up:** Amazon closes accounts that do not make three
qualifying sales within 180 days of approval. At current traffic that window
would likely be wasted. Better applied once Pinterest is sending visitors.

### P3 — Keep the SEO compounding, expect nothing from it this quarter.

The pages are good and they are aging. That is the whole job for now.
