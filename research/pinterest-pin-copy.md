## Week 2 — the easy wins

### 6. calendula.jpg → Cottage Garden Flowers
**Link:** /guides/calendula
**Title:** How to Grow Calendula From Seed

> The easiest seed in the collection, with one detail that matters: calendula
> needs darkness to germinate, so sow it about 6mm deep rather than surface
> sowing. Flowers in six to eight weeks.

### 7. chamomile.jpg → Cottage Garden Flowers
**Link:** /guides/chamomile
**Title:** How to Grow German Chamomile From Seed

> The one mistake that ruins chamomile is burying it — it needs light to
> germinate. Surface sow, keep the surface damp, and pick from about eight
> weeks. Also worth knowing: German and Roman chamomile are different plants.

### 8. yarrow.jpg → Growing Herbs From Seed
**Link:** /guides/yarrow
**Title:** How to Grow Yarrow From Seed

> Yarrow seed needs light, so surface sow and never bury it. When to plant, how
> to keep a tray damp without rotting it, and why the first year is foliage
> only.

### 9. chicory.jpg → Growing Herbs From Seed
**Link:** /guides/chicory
**Title:** How to Grow Chicory From Seed

> Check your local weed list before you sow — chicory has naturalised widely
> and is listed in some places. Then direct sow, because the taproot will not
> survive transplanting.

### 10. calendula-b.jpg → Growing Herbs From Seed
**Link:** /guides/calendula
**Title:** Calendula From Seed: 7-14 Days

> If you want one thing in the garden that simply works, this is it. Sow it in
> the dark, water it, and stop being generous with the soil — calendula flowers
> better on ordinary ground than on rich ground.

## Rules

- **Pin to the guide, never to an affiliate link.** The guide carries the
  affiliate link and its disclosure. Direct affiliate links on Pinterest are a
  policy risk for nothing.
- **One or two a day.** Pinterest natively schedules up to 10 pins, 30 days
  ahead, free on a business account. Queue a week at a time.

  later, write a fresh one.
