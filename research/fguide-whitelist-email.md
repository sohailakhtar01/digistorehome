# Whitelist request — Forager's Guide (`fguide`)

**Send to:** affiliates.gbs@gmail.com
**Subject:** Affiliate whitelist request — Sohail Akhtar (thehomesteadshelf.com)

## Why this email has to be sent before anything is written

The `fguide` affiliate terms say **"Currently, we only accept email marketing."**
A content review site is not email marketing, and the stated penalty for
breaking the rules is an *immediate and permanent ban* — of the Digistore24
account that also carries the Medicinal Garden Kit, which is the only product
currently earning. Publishing first and asking later risks the whole thing to
save a few days.

The second question matters just as much. The terms also say **"refrain from
creating non-positive reviews."** Every review on this site names who a product
is *not* for — the garden kit review says outright that three of its ten species
are difficult and that experienced gardeners should buy seed separately. That
format is the reason the site is worth reading, and it is not going to change
for one product. Better to find out now whether the vendor can live with it.

Note the question is framed as what the site actually does, not as "may I write
negative things." It is an accurate description, and it gives them something
concrete to approve rather than a vague permission to refuse.

## What each answer means

| Their reply | What happens |
|---|---|
| Organic site approved + honest format fine | Write the review. Strongest opportunity found in eight product checks. |
| Organic approved, but strictly positive copy required | **No-go.** Not worth rewriting the site's voice for $26 a sale, and a softened review would not rank against an open SERP anyway. |
| Email marketing only, no exceptions | **No-go.** Do not publish. Move to the Amazon Associates route for the guides. |
| No reply after ~10 days | Treat as no. Silence is not permission when the penalty is a permanent ban. |

## If it is a no-go

Nothing is lost that matters. The research stands, the SERP evidence stands,
and the next move — monetising the growing guides directly, where the traffic
already is — does not depend on this vendor at all.
