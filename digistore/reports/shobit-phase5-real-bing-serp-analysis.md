## #1 — Advanced Amino Formula

### Why it beats the other nine
- **393 indexed results.** Only CaviArgan is thinner, and CaviArgan converts at 2%.
- **The vendor is absent from page 1.** Its two siblings (Mitochondrial, Memory)
  both have advancedbionutritionals.com ranking; Amino does not. There is no
  official page to outrank.
- **Zero authority domains, zero Reddit, zero forums.**
- **Competitors are thin.** Top pages carry `4.9/5 (1)` and `Reviews: 1`.
- **It can be reviewed honestly** — real company, real named formulator, studied
  ingredients, correct FDA disclaimers, no disease claims. Of everything screened
  across 1,381 products, this vendor family is the only one that cleared quality.
- **Horizontal scaling is built in.** Five products from `soundview` reached the
  final 15 — the same site can expand to Mitochondrial, Memory, Pep Tonic and
  Collagen without a new domain, which is exactly the pattern that made
  consumerhealthdigest.com appear in 23 of 30 proxy SERPs.

### Why the SERP looks beatable
Every page-one result is a template supplement-review site with no first-hand
testing, no original data, and in two cases a single rating behind the score.
There is no brand, no institution and no community page to displace — only
undifferentiated affiliate content in a 393-page index.

### Exact query to target first
**`advanced amino formula reviews`** — Bing autosuggest volunteers it, the SERP
is explicitly commercial ("Scam or Legit?", "Is It Worth Buying?"), and the
vendor does not compete for it.

### What to build
A **single deep review page** anchored on what competitors visibly lack:
the actual amino-acid profile and dosing against published research, an honest
treatment of the marketing style, transparent pricing/guarantee, and a clear
statement of who should *not* buy it. Then extend the same site to the four
sibling products.

### What would make this fail
1. **The unresolved Shallenberger licence allegation.** A Trustpilot commenter
   alleges a past California revocation. Unverified in both directions. **If true,
   the one honestly-promotable vendor in the dataset is compromised and this
   flips to NO-GO.** Check the CA and NV medical boards first.
2. **393 results may mean 393-results-worth of demand.** Bing gives no volume, so
   a thin index may reflect thin interest. Single-product revenue could be
   negligible — which is why the portfolio path matters.
3. **7% cart conversion** is mid-tier; traffic must be well-qualified.
4. Pages dated Aug 2026 show competitors are actively working this niche now.

### Confidence: **MEDIUM-HIGH** on the SERP read (real Bing data, unambiguous).
### Confidence: **MEDIUM** on the opportunity (no volume figure; licence question open).
