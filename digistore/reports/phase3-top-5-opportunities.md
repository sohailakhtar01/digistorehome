# Phase 3 — Top 5 Opportunities

All figures: Digistore24 economics from Phase 1/2; Bing SERPs and Google Ads US volume
captured **2026-08-24**. Ranked on the combination of **proven economics + demonstrated
SERP weakness + measured demand** — not on any one of the three.

> None of these is a predicted winner. Each is a candidate whose evidence justifies the
> next stage of validation.

---

## 1. Medicinal Garden Kit — *best overall balance*

**Score 90.32 · $45.70 net/sale · 18% cart conversion · 2.07% cancellation · Promote now**

**Why it wins:** the strongest evidence combination in the study. Its 2.07% cancellation
is the **second-lowest** of any product with published data, and 18% cart conversion sits
in the top decile — this is a funnel that demonstrably converts and does not get refunded.

**SERP verdict — weakest competition observed.** No authority publisher, no major brand,
a three-year-old page still holding #3, and most damning: a **GitHub repository** and a
**ResearchGate "publication"** both ranking as reviews. Parasite-SEO placeholders holding
page-one slots is direct proof Bing has nothing better to rank. Four paid advertisers
confirm real commercial intent.

**Demand — the catch.** `medicinal garden kit` is 110/mo and **falling** (210 in Jan 2026
→ 70 in Jul). `medicinal garden kit review` is 40/mo. Branded demand alone cannot support
a site.

**The actual play:** the surrounding problem-led cluster, not the brand.
`herbal remedies book` = **5,400/mo** ($1.16 CPC), `medicinal herbs to grow at home` =
110/mo. That is **49× the branded review term**. Build the herbal-remedies resource; let
the kit be the monetisation, not the subject.

**Risks:** herbal-medicine claims need careful handling — the honest framing is "seeds
and a growing guide", not treatment. The declining brand trend suggests the offer may be
past its peak. Phase 2 flagged MEDIUM preliminary risk.

**Confidence: HIGH** on economics and SERP; **MEDIUM** on demand durability.

## 2. The Lost SuperFoods — *weakest competition, real adjacent demand*

**Score 87.33 · $27.31 net/sale · 19% cart conversion · 1.70% cancellation · Promote now**

**Why it ranks here:** **1.70% cancellation is the lowest of any shortlisted product**,
with 19% cart conversion — buyers keep it. Five and a half years live.

**SERP verdict — the single weakest competitor set found.** A `.ng` domain ranks **#1 on
a US commercial query**; `bitudi.com` sits at **#2 with a byte-identical title to #1**
(visibly scraped); askaprepper.com holds #4 with a page from **August 2020**. Beating
this on content merit is straightforward.

**Demand:** `the lost superfoods` = 210/mo (**highest branded volume of the three books**,
though declining from 320). `lost superfoods review` = only 10/mo. The real pool is
`long term food storage` = **1,300/mo** ($3.04 CPC) and `survival food to stockpile` =
70/mo ($3.34 CPC) — **130× the branded review term**.

**Risks — two material ones.** Amazon and Goodreads both rank, so **the book is
purchasable outside the Digistore24 funnel entirely**; a buyer can bypass your link. And
a ranking competitor states plainly that the credited author is "a fictional frontman" —
if true, that constrains how the product can honestly be endorsed and must be verified in
Phase 4. Lowest payout of the top five at $27.31.

**Confidence: HIGH** on SERP weakness; **MEDIUM** on economics (channel conflict).

## 3. Tube Magic — *the only product with genuine search demand*

**Score 79.0 · $265.11 net (lifetime) · 1% cart conversion · 8.94% cancellation · Promote now**

**Why it makes the list:** `tube magic` = **1,600/mo — 8× the next-highest branded term**
and the only measured demand that could support a site on brand queries alone.
`youtube automation tools` adds 170/mo at a **$10.16 CPC**, the highest commercial value
of any keyword measured — advertisers pay that because the traffic converts.

**SERP verdict — weak.** A personal **Medium** post ranks #1 and a **Substack** post
ranks #7. When generic publishing platforms hold page one, no committed site owns the
query. The official site is buying ads on its own brand.

**Risks — the most serious of the top five.** Its **1% cart conversion is the weakest of
any shortlisted product**, and 8.94% cancellation is mid-range. Phase 2 flagged that the
$265.11 net is **lifetime value on a $47/month subscription**, not a per-sale payout — the
real front-end commission is roughly $23. Related searches (`tube magic ai free trial`,
`tube magic ai free`) show users hunting free access rather than buying. Software also
dates fast; a review needs continual maintenance.

**Confidence: HIGH** on demand and SERP; **LOW** on economics as advertised.

## 4. Anti-Looter Kit — *best economics, blocked by demand*

**Score 91.9 · $53.01 net/sale · 18% cart conversion · 3.55% cancellation · Promote now*

**Why it is here on merit:** the best risk-adjusted economics in the entire queue —
18% conversion with **3.55% cancellation**, and $53.01 net. Uniquely among the shortlist
it is a **physical, CE-certified product** with a 60-day guarantee, created by a
**verifiable** former CIA officer (Fox News/NBC appearances, published books). Honest
content is far easier to write here than for any supplement.

**SERP verdict — weak and low-trust.** All small affiliate sites; entries **one and two
days old** (churn-and-burn); a `.ng` domain at #6; LinkedIn Pulse at #10; and on an
independent index, **press-release spam** (newswire.com, accessnewswire.com). Confirmed
across two separate search indexes.

**Why it is not #1 — the blocker is decisive.** `anti looter kit` and
`anti looter kit review` **both return no volume data at all** — below Google's reporting
threshold. `home defense kit` is 50/mo; `home security without electricity` is 10/mo.
On a Bing-only strategy this is plausibly a **handful of searches per month**. Excellent
economics aimed at a market that barely exists in search.

Also note `anti-looter-kit.com` is **already registered** — the obvious EMD is gone.

**Risks:** fear-based marketing ("looters", crisis framing) needs restraint; counterfeit
sellers are a known problem in this product's ecosystem.

**Confidence: HIGH** on economics and SERP; **demand is the disqualifier**, not a doubt.

## 5. Encyclopedia of Power Foods — *viable but the weakest of the five*

**Score 86.59 · $29.28 net/sale · 16% cart conversion · 8.97% cancellation · Promote now**

**Why it is included:** solid measured economics (16% conversion) and a genuinely weak
SERP — a **local news site running affiliate content from July 2024** holds #2, and the
EMD `encyclopediaofpowerfoods.com` languishes on page 2.

**Why it ranks last:** no measurable branded volume, and its related searches are
`encyclopedia of power foods pdf`, `power foods encyclopedia pdf`, `power foods pdf` —
**searchers want a free copy, not a purchase**. That is the clearest low-buyer-intent
signal in the study. Cancellation at 8.97% is over 5× the Lost SuperFoods.

It shares the `herbal remedies book` (5,400/mo) demand pool with #1, so it is best
regarded as a **secondary offer inside a Medicinal Garden Kit site**, not a standalone
project.

**Confidence: MEDIUM** overall.

## The strategic conclusion

**The single most important result of Phase 3 is that these SERPs are weak because the
demand behind them is tiny.** Ranking #1 on Bing for `medicinal garden kit review` —
plausibly single-digit Bing searches per month — produces no meaningful revenue no matter
how beatable the competition is.

Both premises behind the stated plan are contradicted by the measured data:

- **"Exact-match domain"** — in *every* observed case the EMD ranks mid-page or buys ads;
  **not one ranks #1**. The domain is not what wins these SERPs.
- **"Bing only, because it's easy to rank"** — the ranking *is* easy. The traffic is the
  problem. Bing-only removes roughly 90% of the available search volume from an
  opportunity set that is already thin.

**What the evidence actually supports:** build a problem-led topical site in the
**herbal-remedies / preparedness** space, where `herbal remedies book` (5,400/mo) and
`long term food storage` (1,300/mo) carry 40–130× the volume of any brand term measured,
and monetise it with the Medicinal Garden Kit and The Lost SuperFoods — both of which
have proven conversion and very low refund rates. Target Bing *and* Google; there is no
cost to ranking on both, and excluding Google forfeits most of the traffic.

## What Phase 4 must resolve before any build decision

1. **Open the sales pages** — still not done; all risk assessment rests on titles and
   SERP snippets.
2. **Verify the Lost SuperFoods authorship claim** — a ranking competitor asserts the
   credited author is fabricated. That is decisive for whether the product can be
   promoted honestly.
3. **Measure the problem-led clusters properly** — full keyword expansion around
   `herbal remedies book` and `long term food storage`, including their SERPs, which have
   **not** been examined and will be harder than the brand terms.
4. **Re-run the 23 unvalidated products** once the SERP API is stable, or via a second
   provider.
5. **Get competitor authority data** — no backlink metrics were available; "weak
   authority" is currently inferred from page quality alone.
