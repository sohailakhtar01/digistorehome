# PART 3 — THE EMD HYPOTHESIS, TESTED PROPERLY

### 1. Were exact/near-exact domains present in this SERP?

**No — zero of ten.**

### 2. Does that absence prove anything?

**On its own, no — and this is the methodological point that matters.** Absence
of EMDs is equally consistent with *"EMDs rank badly"* and *"nobody built a good
EMD site."* Absence alone is weak evidence and I will not treat it as proof.

**But for this product we have something considerably stronger than absence.**

### The buried evidence: the EMD was already built, and it is dead

`medicinal-garden-kit.com` — Internet Archive CDX, **29 snapshots,
2023-01-02 → 2025-07-10**:

| Date | What was there |
|---|---|
| **2023-01-02** | Four `/in.php?clickid=<19-digit>` URLs — **affiliate click-tracker endpoints**, all returning 404. Crawlers do not invent clickid parameters; those links were circulating in the wild. |
| 2023-07 → 2024-07 | Served real pages (HTTP 200; 11KB, 15KB, 16KB) |
| **2024-07-27** | A **thin Google-Sites-style doorway page reproducing Apelian's VSL copy verbatim** — *"The Most Important Thing That's Probably Missing From Your Backyard"*, *"For the last 20 years I've been managing my MS using the natural remedies that I personally grow in my backyard. I went from being pushed in a wheelchair to being fully alive."* — with **"Watch Now"** CTAs |
| 2024-05, 2024-08 | HTTP **403** |
| 2025-07-10 | Last capture, then dropped |

**Someone already ran the exact webinar play — hyphenated exact-match domain plus
a pre-sell page for this exact product. The domain is now dead and available.**

That converts "EMDs are absent" into "an EMD was tried here and did not persist."

**Read it fairly, though:** that site used *scraped, duplicated vendor copy* on a
free-site-builder template. **Its failure is better explained by the content than
by the domain.** This is evidence against the *execution*, not proof against the
*domain concept*.

### 3. Why are the current winners actually ranking?

Not domain relevance — **none of the ten has a keyword-relevant domain.** The
observable drivers are content depth and topical framing (shelfinsider's
herb-by-herb grow-out, rootolife's claims analysis), freshness (six 2026 pages),
and persistence with schema markup (foodnourish, 2023, 4.8/5 rating markup).

**The decisive tell: `biopreneur.com.ng` ranks page 1 of a US commercial query.**
A Nigerian business blog with zero topical, geographic *or* keyword relevance to
"medicinal garden kit." If domain-string relevance carried meaningful weight in
this SERP, that result could not exist.

### 4. Would an EMD provide *any* plausible relevance advantage?

**Yes — plausibly a small one, and I will not dismiss it.** Bing has historically
weighted exact-match domains and URL keywords somewhat more than Google does.
The presenter's claim is not baseless. It is a real, if modest and shrinking,
effect.

### 5. Could the hyphenated `medicinal-garden-kit.com` deliver it?

**Weakly at best.** Hyphenated EMDs are the weakest form — hyphens signal "the
real one was taken," and this one additionally carries a documented
tracker-and-doorway history plus 403s.

**Correction to the option set:** `medicinalgardenkit.com` is *not* vendor-held.
It is **parked on HugeDomains and offered for sale at $2,695.** The unhyphenated
EMD is purchasable — at roughly 200x the cost of a fresh registration.

### 6. Is the advantage large enough to justify it?

**No.** The measured evidence in this SERP says ranking is content-driven. A small
domain-string effect does not offset a burned history, a single-product ceiling,
and the legal exposure below.

### 7. Trademark / brand-confusion risk

**Material and unresolved.**

- **Global Brother SRL holds ~46–47 USPTO trademark filings.** They systematically
  trademark their kit names — **ANTI LOOTER KIT is registered** (serial 98819174),
  and *Anti-Looter Kit is also in our final 15*. HERBNEST and GLOBAL BROTHER are
  also on file.
- I **could not confirm or rule out** a live `MEDICINAL GARDEN KIT` registration
  (USPTO/Justia/Trademarkia all blocked automated retrieval). **Status: UNRESOLVED.**
- A domain reproducing a vendor's product name exactly, used for affiliate
  commercial gain, is the standard UDRP fact pattern. Affiliate respondents
  frequently lose — the *Oki Data* safe harbour requires conditions many
  affiliate sites fail.
- **Registrar availability is not trademark clearance**, as you correctly noted.

**A vendor with 47 filings is exactly the kind of party that enforces.**

### EMD HYPOTHESIS: **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**

The mechanism is plausible and I am not rejecting it in general. It is **not
supported for this SERP**, where the only EMD ever built is dead, ranking is
demonstrably content-driven, and a domain with zero relevance ranks page 1.

# PART 6 — FINAL BUSINESS DECISION

# ➤ B. BUY THEHOMESTEADSHELF.COM

### Why

**The single most important property of this choice: it is not a bet on Medicinal
Garden Kit.** The Apelian gate came back UNCERTAIN. A brand domain would force
the product decision *now*, at the moment we are least certain. A non-brand
portfolio domain **decouples the domain purchase from the product decision** — if
the Apelian disclosure proves too costly to write around, the same domain still
serves Lost Frontier Handbook, Lost SuperFoods, Home Doctor, Anti-Looter Kit and
Joseph's Well. Nothing is stranded.

The EMD would cost the same money and buy a single-product ceiling, a dirty
history, and unresolved trademark exposure against a 47-mark filer.

### Answering your seven questions

**1. Which domain gives the best chance of ranking for the target Bing query?**
Marginally the EMD, in theory. **In this SERP's observed evidence, neither** —
ranking here is content-driven, and `biopreneur.com.ng` proves domain relevance
is not carrying the SERP. The gap is small enough to be dominated by everything
else.

**2. Which best fits the exact-match-domain hypothesis?**
`medicinal-garden-kit.com`, unambiguously. It is the faithful test of the
webinar. **It is also the domain on which that exact test already failed once.**

**3. Which is safest legally?**
`thehomesteadshelf.com`, decisively. No mark reproduced, no confusion, no UDRP
surface, clean history.

**4. Best long-term affiliate expansion?**
`thehomesteadshelf.com`. It covers most of our surviving final 15. The EMD covers
exactly one product and dies with it.

**5. Best chance of actual affiliate sales?**
`thehomesteadshelf.com` — because sales require *ranking content plus a page that
converts*, and an honest review that must disclose an FTC letter converts far
better from a domain that reads as an independent publication than from one that
reads as the vendor's own site. On the brand domain, the disclosure looks like a
betrayal; on a review publication, it looks like credibility.

**6. Biggest risk?**
That **content-driven ranking cuts both ways** — with no domain-keyword help, we
must genuinely out-publish shelfinsider's registered dietitian who grew all ten
plants. That is real work, not a weekend. Secondary: the Apelian disclosure
reduces conversion, and we do not know by how much.

**7. What evidence would prove us wrong?**
- A new exact-match domain entering this SERP's top 3 within 90 days → EMD effect
  is stronger than measured; revisit.
- Our page reaching top 10 but converting below ~1% → the honest disclosure is
  killing conversion; the whole honest-review model needs rethinking.
- A confirmed live `MEDICINAL GARDEN KIT` registration → retroactively confirms
  avoiding the EMD was correct.
- Bing Webmaster Tools showing real impressions but near-zero CTR → the AI answer
  and video block are eating the query; the SERP is less valuable than it looks.

**Asset worth noting:** this session has **Bing Webmaster Tools API access**.
That gives real impression/CTR/position data for our own site once live — the
first genuine Bing measurement available anywhere in this project.

# PART 7 — RANKING EXPECTATION

**No ranking is promised. These are ranges, not predictions.**

### First 30 days
**Realistic: not ranking, or positions ~50–100.** A brand-new domain with zero
history and zero links. Bing indexes faster than Google, and direct URL
submission via Bing Webmaster Tools should get us **crawled and indexed within
days** — but indexed is not ranked. Anyone promising page 1 in month one is
selling something.

### 60–90 days
**Realistic: positions ~11–30 (page 2–3).** A credible shot at the bottom of page
1 (**8–15**) *if* the content is genuinely the best on the SERP and picks up a
few real links. On a 1,010-result index with incumbents this weak, this is
plausible — not assured.

### Longer term (6–12 months)
**Realistic: positions 3–10.** Top 3 is achievable with sustained original work.
**#1 is possible but not predictable, and I will not forecast it.**

### What reaching #1 would actually require

1. Displace `healthreviewnetwork.com`, and out-evidence `shelfinsider.com` — which
   realistically means **buying the kit ($59 + $4.99), planting all ten seeds, and
   publishing first-party germination results with original photography over a
   full growing season.** There is no shortcut that beats primary evidence.
2. **Original photography only** — no stock, no scraped vendor images.
3. **A video.** Bing is surfacing a video block on this query and the incumbents
   have 278–891 views. That block is beatable and would capture real estate above
   most organic results.
4. Earned links / brand signals.
5. Freshness maintenance — a Mar 2023 page still ranks, so persistence is rewarded.
6. **Time: 6–12 months.**
